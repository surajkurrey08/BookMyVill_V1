1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us to deliver a seamless booking and hospitality experience:

- Personal Information: Name, email address, telephone number, and identity details provided during account sign-up or guest check-in.

 2. How We Use Your Information

WorknAi Technology India Pvt. Ltd. uses the collected information for the following legitimate business purposes:
- To facilitate villa searches, bookings, reservations, and customer confirmations.
- To provide your stay pass and communicate essential arrival details (check-in time, location directions, and caretaker contact info).
- To provide dedicated customer support and address your inquiries.
- To notify you about booking status updates, security alerts, and service communications.
- To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraud, security breaches, or unauthorized use of the platform.
- To enhance app functionality and tailor your experience based on preferences.

 3. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We value your privacy and never sell your personal data. We only share information under the following limited circumstances:
- Property Managers & Caretakers: When a booking is confirmed, essential details (guest name, contact number, arrival time) are shared with the property host/caretaker solely to facilitate your check-in and stay.
- Authorized Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors who provide cloud infrastructure, SMS verification, analytics, and payment processing services under strict non-disclosure and data protection agreements.
